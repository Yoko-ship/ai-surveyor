 The purpose of this Law is to regulate relations in the field of personal data.

 Article 2. Legislation on personal data

 The legislation on personal data consists of this Law and other legislative acts.

 If an international treaty of the Republic of Uzbekistan establishes rules other than those provided for by the legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan on personal data, then the rules of the international treaty shall be applied.

 This Law does not apply to relations arising from:

 Предложения по документу Прослушать аудио Получить ссылку из элемента документа

 processing of personal data related to information constituting state secrets;
 Предложения по документу Прослушать аудио Получить ссылку из элемента документа 
 processing of personal data obtained in the course of operational-search, intelligence and counter-intelligence activities, combating crime, law enforcement, as well as in the framework of counteracting the legalization of proceeds of crime.

 confidentiality and security of personal data;

 security of the individual, society and the state.
