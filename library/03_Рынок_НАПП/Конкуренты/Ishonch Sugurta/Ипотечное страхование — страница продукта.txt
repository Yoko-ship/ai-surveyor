Mortgage insurance
 Mortgage insurance
 This type of insurance protects your property from all sorts of risks, from fire to accidents in the water supply, heating or sewerage systems that may occur during the use of the property.
In addition, our company offers you a special title insurance policy that provides protection against the loss of your real estate as a result of the termination of ownership based on a court decision that has entered into force on the following grounds:
- violation of the laws of the Republic of Uzbekistan in the course of previous transactions with insured property;
- presence of errors in the execution of documents confirming ownership (such as a contract, certificate, etc.), as well as documents that serve as the basis for the transaction (power of attorney, certificate, etc.);
- sale of real estate that cannot be transferred to private ownership;
- purchase of real estate that is under arrest, encumbrance or pledge.
